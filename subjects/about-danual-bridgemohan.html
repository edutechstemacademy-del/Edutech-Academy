Why Edutech Academy Exists

Edutech Academy was created to give CSEC and CAPE students a focused learning space with videos, lesson summaries, flashcards, self-assessment quizzes, live revision classes, and printable study support.

  • Clear subject pathways that can start small and expand over time.
  • Technology-supported learning for students who need structure, feedback, and revision practice.
  • Flexible support through online lessons, live classes, and printed study guides.
