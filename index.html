01 About the Camp

The Future Innovators STEM & Robotics Camp is an immersive, project-driven program that transforms curious students into confident creators. Over the course of the camp, participants work with real robotics hardware, write executable code, and tackle open-ended engineering challenges.

Guided by experienced mentors, students learn to think like engineers — iterating, debugging, and collaborating to bring their ideas to life. No prior experience is required; only a willingness to experiment and learn.

Hands-on hardware — build and program physical robots from the ground up.
Real-world problem solving — tackle challenges that mirror today's engineering.
Team-based collaboration — work in crews to design, test, and present solutions.
STEM career exploration — meet professionals and discover future pathways.
